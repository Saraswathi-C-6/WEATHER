About WeatherNow

WeatherNow is your simple and interactive weather forecasting website...

How to Use This Website

  • Search for a City – Type any city name and click the 🔍 icon.
  • Use Your Current Location – Get local weather instantly.
  • View Weather Details – City, time, icon, description, and background.
  • Explore Extra Info – Temp, humidity, pressure, wind, sunrise/sunset.
  • Check Hourly Forecast – Scrollable data for upcoming hours.
  • Responsive Design – Mobile/tablet-friendly interface.
  • Recent Searches – Click inside the input for search history.
